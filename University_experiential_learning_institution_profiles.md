### Birmingham (RG01, UK)

**Review:** Partial — remaining: Shared WebSearch budget exhausted after 14 searches. Remaining: science (Biosciences, Geography/Earth & Environmental Sciences, Mathematics, Psychology); social sciences (INLOGOV/public policy consultancy, Government & Society, Education, Social Policy); arts & humanities (Professional Skills Module current format, cultural-sector projects); other (Law pro bono/clinics, Medicine/Health, Dentistry); computing (MSc/Year 2 team projects with industry); engineering module-level evidence for industry-set group design projects; current-year corroboration for S5/S6; MBA Grow Movement detail. · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 4

**Birmingham** has several distributed live-project routes. Most are **short, extracurricular and centrally run**, with smaller programme-embedded provision in the Business School.

- **Central (Careers Network):**
  - **The Birmingham Project** is open to all first-year undergraduates on the Edgbaston campus. It is optional, non-credit and recorded as pass/fail on the transcript. Interdisciplinary teams spend one full-time week in June on challenges set by 7–8 employer partners in 2026 (e.g. HSBC, Siemens, Transport for West Midlands, West Midlands Combined Authority). Each challenge has an industry professional, and teams finish with a group video presentation to industry and academics. More than 2,000 students have taken part since 2013.
  - The **Masters Consultancy Challenge** is selective and open to taught postgraduates. Interdisciplinary groups work for 3 weeks on a Turner & Townsend challenge and present to an industry and University panel.
  - The **Impact Challenge** is a paid, week-long programme for widening-participation finalists and penultimate-year students. Teams work on employer-set projects, mostly for charities and social enterprises.
- **Business School:**
  - The **Capgemini Community Challenge** is a week at Capgemini Telford in which undergraduate teams consult for charity clients.
  - The **MSc Strategic Marketing and Consulting** includes a group consultancy project for real organisations, but its current-year delivery is unconfirmed.
  - **Masters/MBA Company Projects** tackle live business challenges, but it is unclear whether they are done in groups.
- **Engineering:** MEng group design projects are only described as "industry-linked". The **Chemical Engineering** design project is recorded as Unclear.

**Limits:** the review is **Partial**. The shared search limit was reached after 14 searches. Science beyond Physics, most social sciences and humanities, Law, Health and Education remain largely unsearched, so further departmental routes are likely.

### Bristol (RG02, UK)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 5

Bristol has no university-wide, compulsory employer-project scheme; qualifying provision is distributed across schools, with the Industrial Liaison Office (Faculty of Science and Engineering) acting as the main broker of client briefs.

**Verified current routes.** Engineering Mathematics' *Mathematical and Data Modelling 3* (2026/27) puts groups of 4-6 on three six-week problems, two supplied by industrial partners. The Business School's final-year *Management Consultancy* (EFIM30064, 2025/26) places groups of 4-6 on briefs from outside organisations, mostly charities. The Business School *Micro-Internship* (200 Masters places, 3 weeks, extracurricular) assigns random teams to an industry client. The MSc Marketing applied project (small group, external commercial/third-sector partners) and the Law School's selective Human Rights Law Clinic (student-led teams researching to partner briefs, ~70 members) are also current.

**Credible but not currently evidenced.** Undergraduate Software Engineering Project and MSc Summer Team Project (client-proposed, teams of 4-6) rest on undated ILO pages; Geography's Consultancy Project (3-5 student teams, external clients) appears only in a mislabelled catalogue entry.

**Partial matches.** Civil Engineering MEng Design Project and Data Science Mini-Project use external clients only 'whenever possible' or alongside academic briefs, so C is unclear. Business Analytics, BIE and Data Science for Business routes lack group/partner detail.

Participation is mostly optional or within named programmes; only a few routes are selective. Short formats (City Challenges, one afternoon) are recorded but weak. Faculty PLN live briefs are partner-facing and not tied to identifiable credit units.

### Cambridge (RG03, UK)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 7

**Cambridge has no institution-wide employer-project scheme; provision is concentrated in a few programme-embedded routes.** Cambridge Judge Business School runs the densest set: the MBA Global Consulting Project (4-5 students, ~4 weeks, hosts such as WHO and Uber) and Cambridge Venture Project (5-6 students, ~6 weeks, local businesses), the MFin Group Consulting Project, the MPhil in Management Consulting Project, the undergraduate Management Studies Tripos project (teams of 3-4 for external organisations), and the MPhil in Technology Policy Final Group Project (clients including Cabinet Office, Ofcom, Arm). Each is part of a degree, uses a central client-proposal process with 2026/27 calls, and relies on external organisations as clients who receive the output. Outside business, the Computer Science Tripos Part IB group project is the standout: a compulsory seven-week team project for an assigned industry client, with 2026 clients including Nokia Bell Labs and Bending Spoons. Further routes exist in Engineering (IfM MET/ISMM company projects, ESD MPhil Client Project), Bioscience Enterprise and Public Policy/Conservation Leadership, but these lack dated current evidence. The university-wide i-Teams programme (selective, extracurricular) has teams but internal research clients, so it does not meet the employer criterion. Access is by programme: none is open university-wide. Uncertain: credit weightings, EMBA duration, and current status of the engineering and social-science MPhil routes.

### Edinburgh (RG06, UK)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 5

**Edinburgh** has no institution-wide employer-project programme in operation. The one university-wide scheme, Students as Change Agents (SACHA), survives only as a 20-credit EFI course (EFIE08003 / EFIE08006) in which small interdisciplinary teams work on a challenge set by an external partner and present outputs to it; the Careers co-curricular route is paused. Whether the elective is open university-wide or restricted to the EFI Interdisciplinary Futures MA is unresolved.

The densest provision is distributed in the **Business School**: live-client team consultancies for MSc Global Strategy and Sustainability (teams of 5-6, ten weeks, current in the 2026/27 catalogue), MSc Entrepreneurship and Innovation, MSc Climate Change Finance and Investment, the MBA (consultancy project and a one-week charity consultancy), and an undergraduate Enterprise Consultancy Project (2025/26) for a new local business. Clients propose challenges, attend scoping and presentations, and receive reports; several of these pages are undated or last evidenced before 2025.

**EFI** MSc Creative Industries has a compulsory Client Consulting Project (cultural-organisation client, 2025/26) and a Team Project that mixes client-led and student-led work. The Law School's LLM Human Rights Clinic produces group research with Together for Scottish Government audiences. Design with Data (Design Informatics) is group-based and may use an external organisation's data but partner role is not documented.

Mostly postgraduate and programme-bound; undergraduate access is limited to the Enterprise Consultancy Project and the EFI elective. Engineering, Informatics and the sciences show industry work mainly through individual dissertations or hypothetical clients. Remaining uncertainty: currency of several Business School pages, SACHA eligibility, and further departmental instances.
