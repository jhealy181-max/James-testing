### London School of Economics (RG13, UK)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 16

**LSE** has substantial but mainly **postgraduate, programme-embedded** live-client group project provision, together with a few institution-wide extracurricular routes.

- **Institution-wide:** **LSE100** is compulsory for all first-year undergraduates and includes a 4-5 person group research project. That project is addressed to a self-defined target audience, and no external organisation was evidenced, so it is a near-miss on criterion C. The open qualifying routes are selective and extracurricular: **Future17** (QS consortium; teams of about five work on SDG challenges set by business, charity or NGO partners; open to all undergraduates), the **Volunteer Centre Community Engagement Programme** (teams of 6-7 work as consultants on charity briefs; open to all students) and the **Research Volunteering Scheme** (postgraduate teams answer charity research questions; 10 questions in 2025/26).
- **Embedded client capstones (compulsory within programmes):**
  - **Management:** a sponsored Business Projects framework (GMiM MG488B, CEMS, HRO Links in pairs, MSc Marketing MG4F1), the MiM capstone MG4J4 / London Consulting Project, and the SIE partner project MG4G6.
  - **School of Public Policy:** the MPA capstone PP4B3, where the client marks 20%; also PP4B5 and the Executive MPP capstone PP4B3E with the Civil Service.
  - **International Development:** the DV443, DV453 and DV466 consultancy projects with clients such as UN agencies and NGOs.
  - **Statistics:** the ST498 Data Science capstone, co-supervised by industry partners.
  - **Undergraduate:** the only curricular undergraduate route verified is the **GV343 PPE Capstone** (year 4), with an external client and a report to that client.
- **Partners:** clients define the problems, co-supervise, receive the reports and presentations, and sometimes assess.
- **Uncertain:** credit for Future17; the current status of the law clinics; group size in MA425; whether live clients are used in GY454; and possible further departmental instances (Economics, Social Policy and Media had none found).

### Melbourne (AU02, Australia)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 9

**University of Melbourne** has distributed, mostly faculty-level provision of team projects for external organisations rather than one institution-wide scheme.

- **Compulsory capstones with external clients** are strongest at graduate level: the Master of IT *Software Project* (COMP90082) and Master of Software Engineering *Advanced Software Project* (SWEN90017/18, 'large teams... for an external client'), and the Faculty of Science capstone Industry Project model (teams of 3-5 on partner-submitted briefs) in the Masters of Data Science (MAST90106/7, 2026), Biotechnology (SCIE90015) and Environmental Science. The Master of Engineering capstone (ENGR90037/38) is group-based but involves industry partners only 'where appropriate'; Electrical & Electronic Engineering documents an industry-supervised team route.
- **Elective/selective curricular routes**: Arts *Industry Project* (ARTS30001, small consulting teams for partners who brief and attend presentations, also open as breadth); FBE *Management Consulting* (MGMT30012, teams assigned to client organisations, quota); *Creating Innovative Professionals* (ENGR90039, multidisciplinary teams on sponsor challenges, open to specified programs across Engineering/IT, FBE and Design); Law's Indigenous Legal Advocacy Clinic (partner-briefed, group format unclear in 2025).
- **Extracurricular**: FBE-only Business Innovation Challenge (2 days) and Virtual Industry Projects (25 hours, Practera-matched live clients); cross-faculty SCOUT (CAS Energy, 2026) and IPP O-Lab (status undated).

Partners mostly act as brief-setters/clients; sector mix is rarely documented. No route is university-wide in eligibility except O-Lab (postgraduates). Uncertain: whether COMP30022 clients are external, current status of MGMT90129, MBS Analytics Lab team format, and 2026 detail for several subjects whose current-year tabs were not indexed.
