### Birmingham (RG01, UK)

**Review:** Partial — remaining: Shared WebSearch budget exhausted after 14 searches. Remaining: science (Biosciences, Geography/Earth & Environmental Sciences, Mathematics, Psychology); social sciences (INLOGOV/public policy consultancy, Government & Society, Education, Social Policy); arts & humanities (Professional Skills Module current format, cultural-sector projects); other (Law pro bono/clinics, Medicine/Health, Dentistry); computing (MSc/Year 2 team projects with industry); engineering module-level evidence for industry-set group design projects; current-year corroboration for S5/S6; MBA Grow Movement detail. · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 4

**Birmingham** has several distributed live-project routes. Most are **short, extracurricular and centrally run**, with smaller programme-embedded provision in the Business School.

- **Central (Careers Network):**
  - **The Birmingham Project** is open to all first-year undergraduates on the Edgbaston campus. It is optional, non-credit and recorded as pass/fail on the transcript. Interdisciplinary teams spend one full-time week in June on challenges set by 7–8 employer partners in 2026 (e.g. HSBC, Siemens, Transport for West Midlands, West Midlands Combined Authority). Each challenge has an industry professional, and teams finish with a group video presentation to industry and academics. More than 2,000 students have taken part since 2013.
  - The **Masters Consultancy Challenge** is selective and open to taught postgraduates. Interdisciplinary groups work for 3 weeks on a Turner & Townsend challenge and present to an industry and University panel.
  - The **Impact Challenge** is a paid, week-long programme for widening-participation finalists and penultimate-year students. Teams work on employer-set projects, mostly for charities and social enterprises.
- **Business School:**
  - The **Capgemini Community Challenge** is a week at Capgemini Telford in which undergraduate teams consult for charity clients.
  - The **MSc Strategic Marketing and Consulting** includes a group consultancy project for real organisations, but its current-year delivery is unconfirmed.
  - **Masters/MBA Company Projects** tackle live business challenges, but it is unclear whether they are done in groups.
- **Engineering:** MEng group design projects are only described as "industry-linked". The **Chemical Engineering** design project is recorded as Unclear.

**Limits:** the review is **Partial**. The shared search limit was reached after 14 searches. Science beyond Physics, most social sciences and humanities, Law, Health and Education remain largely unsearched, so further departmental routes are likely.

### London School of Economics (RG13, UK)

**Review:** Complete · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 16

**LSE** has substantial but mainly **postgraduate, programme-embedded** live-client group project provision, together with a few institution-wide extracurricular routes.

- **Institution-wide:** **LSE100** is compulsory for all first-year undergraduates and includes a 4-5 person group research project. That project is addressed to a self-defined target audience, and no external organisation was evidenced, so it is a near-miss on criterion C. The open qualifying routes are selective and extracurricular: **Future17** (QS consortium; teams of about five work on SDG challenges set by business, charity or NGO partners; open to all undergraduates), the **Volunteer Centre Community Engagement Programme** (teams of 6-7 work as consultants on charity briefs; open to all students) and the **Research Volunteering Scheme** (postgraduate teams answer charity research questions; 10 questions in 2025/26).
- **Embedded client capstones (compulsory within programmes):**
  - **Management:** a sponsored Business Projects framework (GMiM MG488B, CEMS, HRO Links in pairs, MSc Marketing MG4F1), the MiM capstone MG4J4 / London Consulting Project, and the SIE partner project MG4G6.
  - **School of Public Policy:** the MPA capstone PP4B3, where the client marks 20%; also PP4B5 and the Executive MPP capstone PP4B3E with the Civil Service.
  - **International Development:** the DV443, DV453 and DV466 consultancy projects with clients such as UN agencies and NGOs.
  - **Statistics:** the ST498 Data Science capstone, co-supervised by industry partners.
  - **Undergraduate:** the only curricular undergraduate route verified is the **GV343 PPE Capstone** (year 4), with an external client and a report to that client.
- **Partners:** clients define the problems, co-supervise, receive the reports and presentations, and sometimes assess.
- **Uncertain:** credit for Future17; the current status of the law clinics; group size in MA425; whether live clients are used in GY454; and possible further departmental instances (Economics, Social Policy and Media had none found).

### Oxford (RG17, UK)

**Review:** Partial — remaining: Search budget exhausted after 13 searches (shared per-turn WebSearch limit). Remaining: arts/humanities/social sciences (Blavatnik MPP, OII, ODID, Humanities, Continuing Education), other (Law, Medical Sciences, Education), remaining science departments and Smith School, Saïd Executive MBA/MSc programmes, confirmation of Careers Service programme status for 2026/27. · **Embeddedness:** Several distributed routes · **Current qualifying schemes:** 3

**Oxford** (partial review) shows qualifying provision in three places, but no institution-wide curricular route.

- **Careers Service (central, extracurricular, selective):** a family of team consultancy programmes for real clients. **The Oxford Strategy Challenge** runs 5-day projects for mixed teams of 4-6 students, from first-year undergraduates to DPhils in any subject (Saïd students excluded); clients define the challenge and receive the recommendations. **Making a Difference** serves charities and social enterprises (50 places a term). **The Student Consultancy** (4-6 weeks) and **The Agency** (term-long marketing briefs) are similar. Most of these pages are now labelled *Programme Paused*, although there are testimonials from Michaelmas 2025 and Hilary 2026, so their current status is unclear.
- **Embedded in degrees:**
  - **Computer Science:** the compulsory second-year **Group Design Practical** (HT2026). Teams of 5-6 build software for industrial sponsors such as G-Research and Palantir, who co-supervise.
  - **MSc Energy Systems:** the whole cohort works as a consultancy for an external stakeholder (2025/26 handbook), although some past stakeholders were internal.
  - **Saïd MBA:** the optional summer **Strategic Consulting Project** for companies and charities. The client provides sponsors and sits on the final presentation panel.
- **Near-misses:**
  - Engineering Science's compulsory 3YP group design project has only occasional company sponsors.
  - The MMSC case studies have no evidenced external client.
  - The legacy **Micro-Internship Programme** is an individual placement.
- **Uncertain:** humanities and social sciences, law and medicine were not searched because the search budget ran out, and it is not confirmed whether the Careers programmes will resume.
